About Campus Connect

Using digital innovation to make student information easier to access.

💡

Our Idea

Campus Connect is a student-focused information platform designed to bring important academic information, university updates and career opportunities together in one accessible location.

💻

Our Digital Skill

Website design and development. We use digital technology to organise information and improve the way students discover useful academic resources and opportunities.

🚀

Our Expected Impact

We aim to save students time, improve access to information, encourage academic organisation and connect students to opportunities beyond the classroom.
